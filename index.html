Nudge

AI Goal Planner for Mac · Big goals, small daily steps

Write a goal in plain words. Nudge asks up to three quick questions, builds a week-by-week plan, adds it to Calendar and Reminders, and checks in with you every day from the menu bar.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store.

How it works

  • Tell it your goal. “Run a 10K in 8 weeks.” Nudge asks where you are and how much time you have.
  • Check your plan. Edit, move, add or delete any task before anything is saved.
  • See it everywhere. Tasks go to Apple Calendar and Reminders, so you can tick them off on your iPhone too.
  • Stay on track. A daily check-in, a streak, and progress for every goal.
  • Get back on track. With Nudge Pro, Nudge offers an adjusted plan when you fall behind. Nothing changes until you approve it.

Private by design

Your goals, plans and tasks stay on your Mac. When AI is on, your goal chat passes through our server to the AI service to write a plan, and our server doesn't keep it. No account needed. Read the Privacy Policy.

Unlimited active goals, 500 AI messages a month, and adjusted plans when you fall behind. $6.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. The free plan includes one active goal and 20 AI messages a month.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email tom.jerrygpt@gmail.com, or use Settings › About › Contact Support in the app. We reply within a few days.

  • Cancel or change your subscription: in the App Store app, click your name, then Account Settings › Subscriptions › Manage. Or Nudge › Settings › Subscription › Manage Subscription.
  • Restore Nudge Pro on another Mac: Settings › Subscription › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Turn AI off: Settings › Privacy. Nudge then sends nothing to the AI service.
  • Delete your data: Settings › Privacy › Delete All Data removes everything from your Mac. To delete the few records on our server (device ID, message counts, subscription status), email us.
  • Calendar or Reminders not updating: check System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars and Reminders, and that Nudge is turned on there.
