Data that stays on your Mac

Your goals, plans, tasks, chat history and settings are stored only on your Mac. We can't see them. If you turn on Calendar or Reminders, Nudge adds your tasks there using Apple's frameworks on your Mac. If those apps sync with iCloud, Apple handles that under Apple's Privacy Policy. Daily check-ins are local notifications made on your Mac.

AI features

Nudge uses AI to ask a few questions about your goal, write your plan, and change it when you ask. Nothing is sent until you choose Allow. You can turn AI off at any time in Settings › Privacy.

When AI is on and you use these features, the following is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, then to Anthropic's Claude API:

  • the messages you write in the goal chat and the coach's replies,
  • the plan being changed (task titles, dates, times, lengths, notes and whether each was done), and
  • your Mac's local date and time, so plans don't start in the past.

Our server doesn't store or log this text. It's not sent with your device ID or anything else that identifies you.

Anthropic processes it to produce the reply. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic automatically deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days and doesn't use them to train its models. It may keep content longer if its automated safety systems flag it as breaking its Usage Policy, or where the law requires. See Anthropic's data retention and Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Please don't include sensitive personal information (like health details, passwords or financial account numbers) in what you type.

Data on our server

To keep the service fair and secure, our server stores only:

  • A device ID. A random identifier created on your Mac by Apple's App Attest service. It proves requests come from a genuine copy of Nudge. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID.
  • Monthly AI message counts for that device, to apply the Free and Pro limits.
  • Subscription status (which plan, whether it's active, and when it ends), taken from purchase information signed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Our server runs on Google Cloud (Cloud Run, with its records in Firestore), in the United States. Like any web server, it keeps standard request logs: the time, the page of our server that was called (for example /v1/chat), the result, your IP address, and the app's name and version as reported by your Mac. They never include what you type. Google deletes these logs automatically after 30 days, and we use them only to keep the service working and secure.

Purchases

Nudge Pro is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple ID under its own terms. Apple sends us signed purchase records so we can turn on Pro. We receive no name, email or card details.

Crash reports

Nudge has no analytics or advertising code. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in macOS, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Nudge also keeps Apple's crash and hang diagnostics on your Mac. They contain technical details only, never your goals or tasks, and Nudge doesn't send them anywhere.

Tracking and advertising

We don't track you across apps or websites, show ads, or sell or share your data with data brokers.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn AI off: Settings › Privacy › Allow Nudge to use AI.
  • Delete your data: Settings › Privacy › Delete All Data removes everything Nudge keeps on your Mac, and can also remove its Calendar and Reminders items.
  • Server data: to see or delete the device ID, message counts and subscription status we hold, email us (Settings › About › Contact Support). These records aren't linked to your name or email, so we may ask for a detail from your App Store receipt to find them. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Calendar, Reminders and notifications: you can withdraw access at any time in macOS System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to or restrict how it's used. Email us to use them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

We process server data to provide the service you asked for (contract), and to keep it secure and fair (legitimate interests). AI processing of what you type is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

International transfers

Our server and Anthropic may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

Nudge isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used Nudge's AI features, contact us and we'll delete any server data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change matters for how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
